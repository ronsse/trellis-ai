## Evidence (current main)

`_ADVISORY_MAX_COUNT` cap in pack_builder.py. `PACK_ASSEMBLED.payload["advisory_ids"]` is the served set; `src/trellis/retrieve/effectiveness.py` reads only that field. **`injected_advisory_ids` has no reader in `src/`**, and `advisories_matched` is only a count, so score-on-match is not a reader swap: it requires a new additive ID field and new measurement semantics.

The deeper defect is independent of the cap. The fitness update blends `0.7*old + 0.3*success_rate`, whose fixed point is deployment pack success rather than advisory lift. A null-effect advisory is therefore unsuppressable whenever the base success rate is at or above the suppression threshold. The exact production rate must be re-derived before quoting a numeric cutoff.

## Contradictions

Not a regression (pre-#499 zero advisories scored). Starvation can be indefinite for never-served rows but not literally never — served rows retain historical presentations. Scope match alone is also too weak for item-scoped advisories: an ENTITY or ANTI_PATTERN row can match a pack that never contains its subject.

## Selected solution / decision

The 2026-09-04 panel split:

- One position recommends one deterministic exploration slot, four ranked slots, served-only attribution, and lift-based suppression with exposed/unexposed sample floors.
- The adversarial position rejects implementation until the owner defines presentation, actuator, exploration cost, and growth semantics; it notes that unrestricted rotation can spend tokens without fixing the absolute-rate actuator.

Owner decision (2026-09-04): **defer**. Retain the current five confidence-ranked served advisories and dispatch no implementation. Keep #503 separate and signal-gated.

## Acceptance criteria (smallest testable)

Owner records:

1. presentation: served, matched, or matched-and-subject-present;
2. actuator: absolute success or `lift_vs_baseline`, including the zero-comparison-arm policy;
3. exploration cost: none, one bounded slot, or another explicitly capped policy with a finite per-pack token/slot budget;
4. growth policy: score, prune, or operator cleanup.

Any implementation preserves `advisory_ids` as exposure, uses additive selection telemetry, pins matched-but-unserved rows as non-exposures, and proves its sampling service rate cannot be silently outpaced by the admitted advisory growth rate. Unbounded full rotation is not an admissible answer.

## Adversarial mutants / failure modes

Silent pick of score-on-match without denominator discipline (#336 pattern); rotate an unbounded tail faster than it can be sampled; condition suppression on `followed_advisory_ids` and introduce post-exposure selection bias; leave the absolute-rate fixed point intact while claiming lift-based learning.

## Disposition acceptance

Reopen design when attributed-feedback volume is materially higher, an advisory-starvation incident is demonstrated, or the owner asks to spend a bounded slot on exploration. A reopened design must answer the four questions above before implementation.
